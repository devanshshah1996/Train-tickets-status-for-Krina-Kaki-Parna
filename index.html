Dining & Kitchen Protocols

  • Exclusivity: The Peacock Lounge is reserved exclusively for the group from Nov 6, 8 AM to Nov 11, 2 PM.
  • Jain & Vegetarian Strictness: Kitchen to remain strictly vegetarian. Thorough sanitization and fumigation required before use. No non-Jain/non-veg cooking or storage allowed during the stay.
  • Personal Chef: The family's personal chef will guide the hotel's team to prepare simple Gujarati dishes.
  • Breakfast: Inclusive breakfast to be served at the Peacock Lounge replicating the regular buffet's Jain options.

Elder Comfort & Accessibility

  • Hydration: 5 litres of boiled and cooled drinking water to be delivered daily at 6:30 AM and 2:00 PM to the rooms of Ramnik Shah, Anil Shah, and Devansh Shah, as well as the restaurant.
  • Bathroom Comfort: Plastic shower chairs/stools and buckets placed in the rooms of Ramnik Shah, Sevantilal Shah, and Anil Shah.
  • Medical Support: The Concierge must have a Doctor-on-call ready, with distances mapped to the nearest well-equipped hospital.

Logistics, Location & Service

  • Room Placement: Requested all rooms to be located close together on the same floor/block. Other guests to be allocated to different blocks for privacy.
  • Gathering Space: Arrangement of 18 chairs/sofa required in either a room courtyard or the Lotus Courtyard for family gatherings.
  • Dedicated Service: Specific group butlers must be assigned who are thoroughly briefed on all of these unique requirements.
  • Proximity: Hotel must be within close drivable distance to a Jain Shwetambar temple.
